# Crypto World's Fair — Official Validation

**Checked:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** VERIFIED where explicitly marked below  
**Scope:** Colosseum Crypto World's Fair main hackathon rules and event page

## Primary Sources

1. Colosseum event page: `https://colosseum.com/worldsfair`
2. Official Hackathon Rules PDF: `https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf`
3. Colosseum hackathon index: `https://colosseum.com/hackathon`

The official rules are the controlling source where page summaries and rules differ.

## VERIFIED — Contest Timing

The official rules state that the contest period:

- starts **September 14, 2026 at 6:00 AM PT**;
- ends **October 12, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT**;
- requires registration before the same October 12, 2026 11:59 PM PT cutoff;
- requires the team leader to upload the Project Submission before the end of the Entry Period.

The public event page independently states that submissions are due **October 12, 2026**.

### Project implication

Panta Signal must treat **October 12, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT** as the hard main-hackathon deadline. Any internal schedule should target an earlier freeze to leave time for final submission checks.

## VERIFIED — Eligibility Framework

The official rules state that entrants must:

- be the age of majority in their country of residence or at least 18 years old, whichever is older, unless Colosseum grants an exception;
- not fall within listed sanctions/restricted-location exclusions;
- not be a sanctioned/prohibited person or otherwise violate applicable restrictions;
- ensure participation does not violate employer or third-party obligations;
- comply with applicable law.

The official rules also exclude certain employees, contractors, directors, officers, and immediate family members associated with the administrator/sponsors.

**Operator-specific eligibility is not asserted by this document.** This file records the rule; an operator eligibility check is a separate confirmation step.

## VERIFIED — Team / Submission Limits

The official rules state:

- an entrant may be a member of only **one team**;
- a team may submit only **one Project Submission at a time**.

The submission should not be treated as confidential.

## VERIFIED — Main Hackathon Judging Criteria

Colosseum judges Project Submissions using the following criteria:

1. **Functionality** — how well the project works and code quality.
2. **Potential Impact** — addressable market and broader crypto-ecosystem impact.
3. **Novelty** — uniqueness of the concept.
4. **UX** — how well blockchain is used to create good downstream user experience.
5. **Open-source** — whether the project is open-source and composes with other crypto primitives.
6. **Business Plan** — viability of a future business and the team's ability to execute.

No weighting percentages were found in the controlling rules.

### Project implication

Panta Signal should optimize not only for a technical demo, but for:

- end-to-end functionality;
- visible differentiation;
- clear user value;
- open-source/reproducible implementation;
- a credible path from hackathon project to product.

## VERIFIED — Language

The official rules require **all submitted Content to be in English**.

This validates the repository rule that public project artifacts and submission-facing materials should be English.

## VERIFIED — Intellectual Property

The official rules state that Colosseum does **not** claim ownership of the Project Submission. Entrants retain the intellectual-property rights they may have in their submission, while remaining responsible for third-party rights and disclosure of open-source/third-party code status.

## VERIFIED — Main Awards / Opportunities

The official event page states a total of **$840,000 in prizes** and **$2.5 million in seed funding** opportunities.

The official rules include, among other awards:

- Grand Champion: **$30,000 Phantom CASH stablecoin**;
- Public Goods Award: **$5,000 CASH**;
- University Award: **$5,000 CASH**;
- next 20 standout teams: **$15,000 CASH each**;
- ecosystem-track awards listed in the rules.

The event page also states that hackathon winners are interviewed/considered for the Colosseum accelerator, whose accepted teams receive **$250,000 pre-seed funding**.

These main-event awards are distinct from the separate Panta API Sidetrack reward documented in the Panta-specific research file.

## VERIFIED — Winner Timing

The official rules state that main-hackathon winners will be announced on or about **December 5, 2026**.

This is separate from the Panta Sidetrack listing, which publishes its own scheduled winner-announcement date.
