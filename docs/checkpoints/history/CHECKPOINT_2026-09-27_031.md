# Checkpoint 031 — One-Market Price Field Types

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Branch:** research/phase1-authoritative-validation-2026-09-27  
**Status:** PRICE FIELD TYPES OBSERVED IN ONE LOCAL SAMPLE

## Completed

The operator locally summarized price-field names, JSON types, and null/present status from list and matching detail snapshots. In both records, yesPrice, noPrice, and primaryYesPrice were strings; primaryNoPrice, secondaryYesPrice, and secondaryNoPrice were null. Numeric values and the market ID were not shared.

## Limits

This is one market sample and does not establish price semantics, freshness, catalogue-wide nullability, or movement. The trade-tape record has zero rows. No price or trade signal is supported yet.
